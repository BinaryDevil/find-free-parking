Parking
South Bellevue Station
  • 1,524 parking spaces
  • 22 ADA parking spaces

Bicycles

Bike parking options available at this station:

  • Bike room: 35 spaces
  • Bike lockers: 8 spaces
  • Bike racks: 20 spaces

Learn more about parking and storing your bike.

Ticket machines

Ticket machines are available on the bus island near each station entrance before heading up to the platform.

Passenger pick-up / drop-off

South Bellevue Station's designated pick-up and drop-off area is located between the center and south entrance to the parking garage. A limited number of short-term parking spaces are also available inside the drop-off loop.

To reach the pick-up / drop-off area from Bellevue Way SE:

  • Turn into the center entrance into the parking garage and turn right into the pick-up/drop-off loop
  • Turn into the southernmost parking garage entrance, then turn left into the pick-up/drop-off loop

Restrooms

This station does not have public restrooms available for passengers.

In-station navigation

This station offers voice, visual, and touch signal instructions to station platforms, elevators, and ORCA card readers through the GoodMaps app.

Download the GoodMaps app in the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
