Announcement Ongoing

From Aug. 10, 2026, through Feb. 15, 2027, parking capacity will be reduced at Kent Sounder Station Garage due to construction.

In effect: Aug. 10, 2026 - Feb. 16, 2027

Routes: S Line

Passengers can continue to park in the garage and access Kent Sounder Station throughout construction.

This project will repair and improve the garage’s concrete structure to help maintain a safe and reliable facility for riders.

Parking Level 5 is now open, and work on Level 4 is scheduled to begin October 13. Please remain aware of construction activities and follow all posted signage when entering or moving through the garage.

We understand that construction may cause some inconvenience to your daily commute, and we appreciate your patience and understanding as this important work moves forward. We’ll continue to share updates as more information becomes available.

Subscribe to Rider Alerts to receive the latest information and updates about construction impacts.

Announcement Ongoing

Starting Monday, March 16, Sound Transit will close Kent Station’s northwest Sounder surface parking lot.

In effect: March 16, 2026 - Jan. 1, 2027

Routes: S Line

The only available spots for this lot will be three ADA parking spots. This closure will last the duration of this project phase. We expect this phase to be completed in December 2026. We encourage drivers to use the existing Kent garage at W Smith St and 1st Ave N.

For more information on the project, visit the Kent Station Parking and Access Improvement Project page.

Metro Flex

Metro Flex is a neighborhood transit service providing on-demand rides to and from this station. Passengers can download the app and request a ride anywhere in the service area for the same cost as a King County Metro bus trip.

Learn more about Metro Flex

Parking

996 parking spaces are available for Sound Transit passengers.

Sound Transit's permit parking program is currently suspended at this station.

Bicycles

Bike parking and storage options available at this station:

  • Bicycle lockers
  • Free bike racks

Learn more about parking and storing your bike

Ticket machines

Ticket machines are available on the Track 1 platform, west side of the tracks, and on the Track 2 platform, east side of the tracks.

Learn more about how to use ticket machines

Restrooms

This station does not have public restrooms available for passengers.

Public art

To view art at this station, check out our STart photo gallery.
