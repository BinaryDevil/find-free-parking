Park & Rides

Search for Park & Rides in our service area
Use the list to find facilities by community or by your bus route, or search using the map and clicking on a pin.

Transit centers and Park & Rides are located throughout our service area in Snohomish County and serve as public transit hubs. Parking facilities are available for Community Transit customers, carpoolers, Vanpoolers, and other authorized people. Unauthorized vehicles and activities may be subject to removal.
