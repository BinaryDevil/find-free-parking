Permit parking program is suspended

King County Metro's Permit Parking Program for transit customers at Metro's Park and Rides is suspended until further notice. You may park at Metro park-and-rides for no charge-first come, first served.

Park-and-rides that offered parking permits prior to the program suspension currently do not need permits to park. This does not include non-transit use requests that are reviewed through Special Use Permits (General).

Select Sound Transit parking facilities offer parking permits. For more information, visit Sound Transit's website.
